DealLens 5.0: the revamp (Live status, Pricing check, Amazon vs Flipkart)

What changes
- Three tabs, matching the approved mockups:
  1. Live status: listings live out of total for Amazon and Flipkart, 30-day bars, a "Down now"
     list (longest-broken first, with how long it's been down), and every family's 30-day history.
  2. Pricing check: pick the selling price column from your sheet, Sale (column minus %, with
     per-family %) or Normal day (exactly the column), checked to the exact rupee.
     New price columns in the sheet are detected automatically and offered with a before/after preview.
  3. Amazon vs Flipkart: same price on both, price differs, live on one only.
- One "Check all" button on every tab, plus "Recheck problems" on Live status.
- A listing counts on a platform when the sheet has its ASIN / FSN, unless the sheet's Status says
  that platform is "Pending" (the 4 Lizon rows on Flipkart today).
- Removed: deal-file uploads, the Changes tab and banner. Nothing is deleted from the database.

STEP 1 - Supabase (DispatchLens project) > SQL Editor > New query:
  paste all of 004_deallens_revamp.sql and press Run. Safe to run twice.
